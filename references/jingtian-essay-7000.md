## 段 1 · 度量衡开场

> My girlfriend Jing Tian. The weight of one egg is 3.5 micrograms. The weight of fifty million US dollars in cash is 2.5 tons. What Jing Tian asked me over the phone from Montage Laguna Beach was the latter, and what she put up as collateral was the former.

## 段 2 · 包下整层

> She went there for egg retrieval. The surrogate child was expected to be born in 2027 — if things went quickly, it could still be born in the Year of the Horse. She proposed it herself. When she got to the clinic, she said: no fifty million, no retrieval. Montage Laguna Beach sits on a sea cliff, with the Pacific Ocean below. I booked the entire floor for thirty days at one million dollars, for her privacy. She stayed eight days and left.

`<!-- 技法：结局前置 + 数字精确律 + 删除高潮 -->`

## 段 3 · 服务者复调

> For the remaining twenty-two days, the hotel's housekeepers came up every day as usual. My assistant told me — when she went to return the keys, she ran into one of them: a Mexican woman pushing a cart, going room by room, changing towels, wiping counters, tucking in the duvet corners, stepping out and closing the door. It took her two hours to do the whole floor. My assistant asked her: no one's staying, why do you still do it? She said: the registration says someone is checked in.

## 段 7 · 称呼情节 + 双线节拍

> Xiaonei required a message. I wrote, deleted, rewrote, deleted again — took about forty minutes. Finally I sent: 'Hello.' She never accepted. She probably never even used Xiaonei. In the years that followed, every time I did something, I asked myself the same question: at what point would she accept?

`<!-- 技法：双线节拍（"我说好"暗线）+ 称呼情节（Hello → Jing Tian → 她）-->`
